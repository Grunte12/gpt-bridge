# Product or e-commerce mockup

- Identify the product, buyer, selling context, and whether the asset is a hero,
  detail view, packaging mockup, catalog image, lifestyle scene, or ad.
- Specify exact geometry, materials, finish, colorway, camera angle, scale,
  lighting, contact shadow, surface, and background.
- When a source product exists, use `worker edit`; preserve silhouette, logos,
  labels, proportions, controls, and defects that identify the real item.
- For packaging, supply every visible word as exact copy and state its face and
  hierarchy. Never let the model invent claims, certifications, ingredients,
  prices, or legal copy.
- Check reflections, seams, labels, ports, hands, scale, and physical support.
  Forbid duplicate products, floating objects, warped packaging, fake badges,
  unrelated props, and watermarks.
