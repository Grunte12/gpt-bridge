# Illustration or concept

- Name the medium: editorial illustration, watercolor, ink, painterly concept,
  clay render, 3D, pixel art, or another requested treatment.
- Define silhouette, focal subject, depth, palette, texture, and mood.
- Preserve the user's story. Do not invent characters, props, logos, or copy.
- State whether the output should feel polished, handmade, graphic, cinematic,
  playful, restrained, or production-ready.
