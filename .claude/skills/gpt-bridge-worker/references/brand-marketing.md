# Brand, campaign, or social asset

- State the audience, channel, campaign objective, single message, asset size,
  safe zones, call to action, and required variants.
- Separate immutable brand assets from flexible art direction. Use supplied
  logos or product images as references; never ask the model to redraw a logo
  that must be exact.
- Quote all visible copy and specify hierarchy and placement. Keep copy short
  enough to verify visually.
- Define a campaign system: palette, typography character, imagery, texture,
  margins, logo clear space, and recurring motif. Vary composition without
  changing identity.
- Do not invent endorsements, prices, discounts, claims, legal lines, hashtags,
  or partner marks. Forbid fake UI chrome and watermarks.
